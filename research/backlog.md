### 2b: final SCALE_HI decision rule (pre-registered 2026-11-23, owner-approved, written before any new run)
Candidates: SCALE_HI 1.02 and 1.05, each against live 1.15. No other values will be tried. A candidate passes only if all four hold:
- (a) the pooled paired ticket sign test (tickets that went miss->land vs land->miss) over all 9 leak-free seasons, 2018-19 to 2026-27, has p < 0.01;
- (b) tickets are not below 1.15 by more than 3 in any season;
- (c) the Ladder, judged only on seasons with real player prices (2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25), loses no more than 3 hits in total against 1.15 and has no lower profit in units. 2024-25 uses the ESPN BET X+ rows; 2022-23 and 2023-24 use whatever ESPN's books posted (fetched for this test), priced the way the live code would;
- (d) the live lock-snapshot minutes bias for 24+ minute players is between -0.5 and +0.5 (the measure in the skeptic's SCALE_HI question below: +1.09 at 1.15, -0.15 at 1.05, -0.90 at 1.02, 2026-11-09 to 11-22; refreshed with any newer snapshots).
If both pass, pick the one with live bias closer to 0. If neither passes, SCALE_HI stays 1.15 and the idea is rejected for good. Seasons 2019-20 to 2023-24 were in the 1.02 fit, so (a) over 9 seasons is not a clean holdout for 1.02; the rule is fixed anyway.

### Replay leak: data found (data-scout, 2026-11-23)
Source: the NBA's official injury report PDFs, static files, no login, plain GET with a normal User-Agent (the nba.com pages and official.nba.com block bots, so use only the ak-static host and do not fight the rest). `https://ak-static.cms.nba.com/referee/injury/Injury-Report_YYYY-MM-DD_HHPM.pdf` (hour of the report stamp, which is :30 past; e.g. `_03PM` = stamped 03:30 PM ET). A missing file answers 403 (AccessDenied XML), not 404.
- Format change on 2025-12-22: the name becomes `_HH_MMPM` (e.g. `_04_00PM`, every 15 minutes from 09:00 AM, stamp exact). 2025-12-19 is the last legacy day (hourly), 2025-12-21 legacy too. Today's reports exist (2026-11-20 `_03_30PM` returns 200). The repo `mxufc29/nbainjuries` (MIT-like, 48 stars) documents the same URL rules; use it only as a reference, it needs Java/tabula.
- Cadence by era: 2018-12-18 onward 3 files a day (`01PM`, `05PM`, `08PM`; nothing before 2018-12-18, so 2018-19 starts mid-December); 2022-12 onward hourly 09AM-11PM (hourly probably from 2021-22, I only checked 2022-12-14 and later); from 2025-12-22 every 15 minutes. Coverage of days with games, checked by HEAD: 2024-25 165/165, 2025-12-22 to 2026-04-15 106/106, 2025-10-20 to 2025-12-21 all present, 2018-19 from 2018-12-18 all present.
- Lock mapping (ET = Madrid minus 6 h, but 5 h for the weeks when only one side has switched clocks): lock 22:00 Madrid = 16:00 ET: use `_04_00PM` (new era), `_03PM` (hourly era, stamped 3:30), `_01PM` (3-a-day era, stamped 1:30). Ladder lock 19:00 Madrid = 13:00 ET: `_01_00PM`, `_12PM`, `_01PM`.
- Columns: Game Date, Game Time, Matchup, Team, Player Name ("Last,First", suffix glued: `McCullarJr.,Kevin`; the older PDFs have "Last, First"), Current Status, Reason (older ones also Category and Previous Status). Status in Out / Doubtful / Questionable / Probable / Available; `NOT YET SUBMITTED` for a team that has not filed. G League / two-way assignment rows are listed as Out (drop them, they are not rotation players). Players absent from the report are healthy as far as the team says.
- Parser: pdfplumber text lines, split at the first comma, first token after it is the first name, first status word after it is the status; match on a compact name (`norm_name` rules, drop jr/sr/ii/iii/iv, no spaces): `pc.startswith(first) and left.endswith(pc[len(first):])`. Prototype and fetch cache: `~/.cache/tipoff_injury/scripts/ir.py` (fetch, parse), `ev3.py` (the measurements below), PDFs in `~/.cache/tipoff_injury/pdf` (4.5 MB for the sample, venv with pdfplumber in `~/.cache/tipoff_injury/venv`). Name match to our players: 97% of non-G-League entries on 5 dates of 2024-25 (misses are nicknames and spelling); the report lines wrapped over two rows lose their status, which is part of the 3% unmatched.
- Reliability, 14 sample dates (4 old eras, 5 in 2024-25, 5 in 2025-26), "rotation players" = averaging 20+ minutes over their last 5 games, 10+ games, on a team playing that day; "played" = minutes > 0 in our box score. At the 16:00 ET lock report: Out 206, played 0.0% (never); Doubtful 13, played 0%; Questionable 108, played 52.8% (2024-25 47.6%, 2025-26 53.8%, 2022-24 70.6%); Probable 45, played 91%; Available 22, played 100%; not listed 1,299, played 96.5% (so 3.5% of unlisted rotation players still sit: late scratches, rest, unfiled teams). At the 13:00 ET (Ladder) report: Out played 0.7%, Doubtful 0%, Questionable 51%, Probable 88%, unlisted 92.2%. By the last report before tip (about 18:30 ET) the same rotation players: Out 252 (0%), Questionable 62 (46.8%), Available 70 (97%), unlisted 98.4%. So at the 16:00 lock "Out" catches about 65% of all rotation absences, any listed status about 86%; the rest are unlisted or late. Report timing matters: many teams file late (at 3:30 PM ET about 12 of 22 teams had not filed on a typical 2025-02 night), so the lock-time report is much emptier than the final one.
- Cost for everything: one PDF per game day at the lock time (plus one at 13:00 ET for the Ladder if wanted): about 1,000 days from 2018-12-18 to now at 35-90 KB each, around 60-80 MB for the lock report, 0.3 s polite pause each = about 6 minutes of fetching and a few minutes of parsing per pass. Not downloaded yet beyond the 82-PDF sample.
- Other sources tested: ESPN core and site injuries endpoints (`.../teams/{id}/injuries`, `site.api.espn.com/.../nba/injuries`) hold only the current status (season 2027, no history). `data/history/injury_log.csv` is our own live log from ESPN, only from November 2026. Kaggle ghopkins nba-injuries 2010-2018 has injury events, not game statuses, and ends before our data. Not usable: official.nba.com season pages (blocked by bot protection, untouched). Wayback Machine pages for Rotowire/CBS not tested, not needed.
